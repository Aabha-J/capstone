Chiplet Interconnect Simulator

Configure a topology, generate repeatable random traffic, and compare interconnects interactively.

Simulation inputs

The seed makes the traffic matrix reproducible. Reuse the same seed and packet settings to compare topologies. Duration is in ns.

System-wide interconnect

The selected technology and parameter values are shared by every connected link. Electrical aggregate rate = transfer rate × lane width × lane count. Optical aggregate rate = rate per wavelength × wavelengths per fiber × physical fibers/waveguides. Effective bandwidth is the lower of aggregate rate and bandwidth density × area.

Topology links

Links are bidirectional. Distances and maximum reach are in mm; default is 1 mm. Change distances per link or reset all to the configured default. Links beyond known maximum reach are excluded.
